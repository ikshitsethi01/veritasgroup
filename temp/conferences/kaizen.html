Kaizen For Change [変化のための改善]

Volunteer InstagramLinkedin

LOCATION: Faridabad, Haryana, India
EMAIL: kaizen@veritasgroup.in

In a world where vulnerable communities struggle to access basic needs, our organization stands as a beacon of hope. We provide aid, support, and resources to marginalized groups, addressing immediate needs like food, shelter, healthcare, and education. Empowering individuals and communities to break the cycle of poverty and inequality is our ultimate goal. Through sustainable solutions, self-sufficiency, and resilience, we strive to create a harmonious global community. Kaizen uplifts underserved communities through education and skill-building programs too. With compassion, empowerment, inclusion, integrity, and collaboration as our guiding values, we envision a future where everyone thrives, contributes, and enjoys equal access to quality education, healthcare, and economic opportunities.

Buy a Rose Initiative

We set up a stall at the maiden edition of the Verizon Model United Nations, where we sold over 100 roses at 30 rupees each. This initiative not only encouraged people to spread love by sharing roses but also contributed to a fundraiser aimed at supporting underprivileged communities. This event was followed by a launch event of Kaizen For Change where we introduced the project to the public for the first time and talked about our Vision and Mission.

Plantation Drive

We organized a plantation drive involving 25 junior school students from our community. Together, we planted over 50 saplings and plants across the designated area. The event not only engaged the children but also raised awareness about the impacts of global warming and emphasized the importance of planting and nurturing trees for a sustainable future.

Blood Camp

In the November of 2024, we organized a Blood Donation Camp in partnership with the Lion's Club and Shivaji Maharaj Charitable Hospital. The event saw an overwhelming response, with over 125 donors stepping forward to contribute. Each donor was provided with certificates and refreshments as a token of appreciation. To ensure the safety and efficiency of the process, thorough medical checkups were conducted, and blood was collected safely across all blood groups.

Mind Over Marks

In collaboration with The Mind Haven, we organized "Mind Over Marks," a free interactive workshop led by Harshita Paul on February 2nd, just as students were entering the final exam season. This initiative aimed to help students alleviate exam-related stress and engage in meaningful activities highlighting the importance of mental health and wellbeing. The event successfully brought together over 60 students from various grades, fostering a supportive environment for their emotional and academic growth.
